## The problem

The thing a content site fears most is looking finished: the page opens, but the case study has no screenshots, no repository and no verifiable metrics. Relying on human memory, things are easily missed.

## The approach

Five items are computed for each published project:

1. GitHub source;
2. Cover;
3. Gallery;
4. Metrics;
5. Demo / paper / docs.

The dashboard summarizes completeness and lists the missing items, with links that jump straight to the corresponding CMS entry. A gap is not a build error but a content todo; projects without evidence do not get fabricated links or screenshots.

## Why at build time

Health is a function of the content and needs no database. Astro reads the content collection at build time, computes the statistics and outputs them statically; the admin needs no extra API, and no token is ever put into the browser.

## Limits

- It can only check "whether it exists", not judge "whether it is good".
- Metrics and links must be supplied by the author; the scripts do not guess.
- Once older content is filled in, the next build updates automatically.

## Implementation index

- `src/pages/admin/index.astro`: evidence completeness and the missing-item list.
- `scripts/content-audit.mjs`: a command-line evidence table.
- `scripts/health-report.mjs`: scheduled health reports.
