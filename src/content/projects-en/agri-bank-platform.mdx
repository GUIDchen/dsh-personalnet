## Background and problem

Supply and demand information for agricultural products is scattered across farmers, cooperatives and distribution channels, which makes matching inefficient. The platform needed to integrate multi-source data to support supply-demand matching and analysis.

## Constraints

- Data comes from a variety of sources, with inconsistent formats and update frequencies.
- Network and device conditions are limited in rural settings, so the platform had to stay lightweight and usable.
- The data involves business information, so the scope of data visibility had to be controlled.

## Approach and architecture

The data pipeline is organised as "collection — cleaning — integration — analysis": a unified data model is established, information is organised by subject (farmers, cooperatives, channels), and queries and analysis are then designed around the matching scenarios.

## Key decisions

- **Unified subject model**: define the subjects and their relationships first, then talk about features.
- **Incremental updates**: design update strategies according to each data source's characteristics, avoiding full reruns.
- **Analysis serves the scenario**: only build metrics related to matching, rather than chasing breadth.

## Results and measurement

Completed the collection, integration and analysis of agri-business data, supporting the data needs of precise matching scenarios and providing a data foundation for platform features.

## Technical details

- Used Pandas/SQL for collection, cleaning and field alignment of agri-business data.
- Established supply-demand matching fields and statistical definitions for agricultural products and agri-business resources.
- Covered data ingestion, integrated analysis and delivery of matching scenarios on the platform side.

## Reflection

The key to integrating multi-source data is master data management. If I did it again, I would establish master data and de-duplication rules before developing the analysis features.
