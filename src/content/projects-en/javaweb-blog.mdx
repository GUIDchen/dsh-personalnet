## Background and problem

The graduation project required going through the full software engineering process end to end: from requirements analysis to database design, coding, testing and documentation, rather than just writing a few pages.

## Constraints

- Solo work over roughly 8 months, so scope had to be controlled.
- The tech stack was limited to the JavaWeb ecosystem.
- The system had to be demonstrable, deployable and backed by complete documentation.

## Approach and architecture

A classic three-tier structure: presentation layer (JSP/Servlet), business layer, and data access layer (MySQL). The core modules were article management, search and comments, with permissions distinguishing visitors from administrators.

## Key decisions

- **Scope convergence**: no multi-user support and no rich text editor, to keep the core path high quality.
- **Database first**: design the table structures and relationships before writing business code.
- **Documentation in step**: requirements, design and test documents were updated alongside development and eventually became the thesis.

## Results and measurement

Completed the article publishing, search and comment modules; the system passed functional testing and could be deployed and run, resulting in a graduation thesis of more than 19,000 characters together with supporting documentation.

## Technical details

- Split controllers, views and data access according to the Servlet/JSP MVC layering.
- Designed the table structures and indexes for articles, comments and search.
- Covered CRUD, paginated search, comment submission and frontend-backend integration.

## Reflection

This was the first time I completed an "engineering" project rather than a "homework" exercise. If I did it again, I would use version control and automated tests instead of manual regression, and I would validate deployment earlier.
